## 6. P11 / SELF-365 — what "a month of operation" means on a greenfield deploy

PRD §3.4(c) verbatim: *"The F/CTO has run their monthly review cycle on V1 alone — without consulting the existing manual-spreadsheet system as a working source — for N = 2 consecutive months … the second month surfaces month-boundary edge cases — quarter-end estimated-tax computation, year-end NAV anchor rollover, monthly-report regeneration semantics."* SELF-365 AC (c): *"N=2 consecutive months of monthly report generation + commentary authoring + RLS clean."* MILESTONE-FRAMING §8.2: the residual Google Sheets views read from V1.x during transition; §8.3(c) terminates them at V1.final.

**Proposed definition (PM; F/CTO ratifies at the sitting).** A calendar month **M counts** when all of: (1) V1 held the tenant's connected/manual accounts for the whole of M (so M is the first *full* month after V1.5 deploy, not the deploy month); (2) the 1st-of-(M+1) cron fired and created M's pending report — evidenced by the per-generation audit row (D-8), trigger = cron, `data_as_of` = last day of M; (3) the user **authored** commentary for M (see the ruling below) and finalized; (4) the user exported M's PDF at least once (the Lock 13 path exercised end-to-end); (5) the §2.6.6 battery (SELF-362) was green on the tree that generated M; (6) the F/CTO attests no reconciliation against the sheet was needed for M — the one criterion that is a statement, not a measurement, and §3.4's own failure mode. **Two consecutive** = M and M+1 both pass. Earliest V1.final on a greenfield deploy is therefore the close of the second full month after V1.5 ships — the calendar, not the roster, sets it.

**What V1.final needs FROM V1.5 to be measurable:** the audit row per generation carrying trigger source and `data_as_of` (Lock 11 mod #2 / Decision 19 shape) — without it (2) is unmeasurable; a durable **authored-vs-skipped** fact per report (see ruling); `generated_at` on the final row; and the battery's verdict recorded per SELF-269 precedent.

**`⟨RULING⟩` Does an explicitly-skipped commentary month count toward N = 2?** Options: **(A)** No — §3.4(c) and SELF-365 both say *commentary authoring*; a month with all four sub-sections skipped exercised the queue but not the editor. **PM lean.** Losing side: a month with genuinely nothing to rebalance fails the gate for a reason unrelated to V1's correctness, and could add a month to V1.final. **(B)** Count it if at least one sub-section was authored. **(C)** Count any finalized report. Either way the fact must be persisted (a skip must be distinguishable from four empty strings), which is a V1.5 AC on A1/P4, not a V1.final one.
